How AyurMitra Operates

01

Multilingual Query Parsing

Normalizes English, Hindi, Kannada, Hinglish, and Kanglish while safeguarding Sanskrit Ayurvedic terminology and botanical identities.

02

Hybrid Retrieval (BM25 + TF-IDF)

Combines lexical term exactness and sparse semantic coverage across 110,000+ passages with exact botanical-alias boosting.

03

Grounded Answer Synthesis

Extracts verifiable passages or prompts local Ollama LLMs to cite retrieved evidence records, abstaining when evidence is insufficient.

04

Safety Triage & Guardrails

Screens for red-flag symptoms (e.g., chest pain, respiratory distress), provides emergency alerts, and enforces non-diagnostic boundaries.
