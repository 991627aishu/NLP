# Ayurvedic Diagnostic Methodology

## Rogi Pariksha
Examination of the patient involves Ashtavidha Pariksha: Nadi, Mutra, Mala, Jihva, Shabda, Sparsha, Druk, and Akruti.

## Roga Pariksha
Examination of the disease involves Nidana Panchaka: Nidana (etiology), Purvarupa (premonitory symptoms), Rupa (manifest clinical symptoms), Upashaya (exploratory therapy), and Samprapti (pathogenesis).

## Chikitsa Sutra
Therapeutic principles encompass Shodhana (purification) and Shamana (pacification).
